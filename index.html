The band

Two people.
One very long cable.

Fifty Five Pawns is Ethan and William. They met in the school orchestra around 2023, started writing songs instead of practising, and have not really stopped since.

The songs move fast on purpose: a rap verse, a piano line nicked from a lullaby, a chorus big enough for a room of strangers to shout back, then a breakdown that goes on slightly too long. Everything is played live by two people and a lot of pedals.

There is no album yet and nothing released. Just a growing pile of demos, a few half-finished songs and a lot of voice notes.
